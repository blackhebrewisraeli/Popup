THE WORKSPACE

One question. A clearer path to evidence.

01 / RESEARCH

Start with dated candidates.

Research opens on a daily shortlist of Binance Spot pairs ranked by 24-hour quote volume: a liquidity ranking, not a score. Model scan (1D) then shows factors, Risk clauses, and coverage.

Research workflow →
02 / MANUAL SPOT

Record what you did.

Trades are placed by hand on Binance, then recorded as experiments: source scan, rules, linked fills, observations, and close. Popup evaluates no rule.

Manual Spot records →
03 / ACCOUNT

Keep the boundary clear.

The owner-only Binance Spot connection reads balances and finite execution history without placing orders. Paper Desk simulations are paused and stay read-only.

Read-only account scope →

The daily batch is collected once a day by a scheduled Edge Function. TradingView development scans run locally through an authenticated agent at the owner's request. They are not an unattended hosted scanner. The hosted app, market-data streams, alerts, and account reads have separate capabilities.
